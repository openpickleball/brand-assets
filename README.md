# brand-assets

Logos, colors, fonts, and images for OPEN, the Open Pickleball Evaluation Network. Use them for promotional materials, slides, posters, and press.

## Before you use anything here

The OPEN name and logo are not covered by an open license. See [How OPEN is licensed](https://github.com/openpickleball/.github/blob/main/LICENSING.md). Use of the name and logo needs permission until brand guidelines are published here. If you are not sure, ask first.

Only say what is true today. No OPEN rating is live yet, so do not present anything as an OPEN rating.

## What is here

| Folder | Contents |
| ------ | -------- |
| `logo/` | The OPEN mark as SVG in four colors: white, black, ivory, and ink (`#17150f`) |
| `icons/` | App and browser icon: SVG, 48 px favicon, 180 px touch icon, 192 and 512 px app icons, 512 px maskable icon |
| `images/` | Social share image (1200 x 630) and the horizon artwork in wide, landscape, and portrait crops |
| `fonts/` | Krona One and Instrument Sans (web font files) with their SIL Open Font License texts |
| `colors.json` | The black and ivory color palettes |

## Which logo to use

- On a black or dark background: `open-mark-white.svg` or `open-mark-ivory.svg`.
- On an ivory or white background: `open-mark-ink.svg` or `open-mark-black.svg`.
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects.

## Type

- The OPEN wordmark is set in Krona One, uppercase, with wide letter spacing (about 0.1em).
- Headlines and body text use Instrument Sans.
- Both fonts are free under the SIL Open Font License. The license texts are in `fonts/`.

## Source

These files were taken from the [frontend](https://github.com/openpickleball/frontend) repository. The logo is generated by `design/tools/export-logo.py` there. If the logo changes, update it in `frontend` first, then copy it here.
